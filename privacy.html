Privacy Policy

Vaughan Family App · Effective September 29, 2026

Vaughan Family App is a small, private text-message service run by Matthew Vaughan to help one family arrange rides for an elderly relative. It is not offered to the public. Only people who have personally agreed to take part receive messages.

What we collect

  • Your name, mobile phone number, and your role in the family (for example, driver).
  • Appointment titles, dates, times and places from the family's shared calendar.
  • The text messages sent to and from you through the service, including your replies.
  • If the family coordinator texts a photo of a paper calendar, the photo is read to find appointments and then deleted from our texting provider. The appointments it produced are kept.

We do not collect medical records, medications, payment card details, or government ID numbers, and we ask family members to keep appointment titles generic.

How we use it

Only to run the service: asking drivers about rides, sending ride reminders, and keeping a history of what was sent so the family can see what happened.

Who it is shared with

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We use a small number of service providers that process information only to run the service: Twilio (delivers text messages), Google (the shared calendar), Render (hosting and storage), and an AI service that reads photos of the paper calendar when the coordinator sends one. We do not share your information with anyone else.

Keeping it safe

The administration page is protected by a password and is used only by the person who runs the service and the family coordinator. Text messages are not encrypted in transit through phone carriers, so please do not put private details in your replies.

Your choices

Reply STOP to any message and you will receive no more. You can also ask the person who invited you to remove your number and delete your message history, and it will be done.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. For questions, contact the family member who invited you to the service, or reply HELP to any message.
