Vaughan Family App · Effective September 29, 2026

Vaughan Family App is a private text-message service run by Matthew Vaughan to help one family arrange rides to appointments for an elderly relative. It is available only to relatives, friends and neighbors who have agreed to take part.

SMS Terms

  • Program: Vaughan Family App ride coordination.
  • What you will receive: Requests asking whether you can drive to an appointment, ride reminders, and replies to messages you send. No marketing or promotional messages are sent.
  • How you join: You are asked in person or by phone and must say yes before your number is added. A confirmation text is sent when it is.
  • Frequency: A few messages a month. This varies with how many appointments the family has.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time. You will receive one confirmation and no further messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP, or contact the family member who invited you.
  • Carriers: Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.
  • Agreeing to receive messages is not a condition of anything else.

What the service is, and is not

The service is a coordination tool. It does not provide medical advice, and it is not for emergencies. In an emergency, call 911. Rides are offered by family and friends on their own initiative; the service does not book, pay for, or insure any ride.
